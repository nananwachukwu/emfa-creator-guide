
## The EU Media Act, Decoded for Creators

An interactive legal analysis of the **European Media Freedom Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1083) — breaking down what it means for content creators, who it applies to, and when a creator becomes a media service provider under EU law.
View here: https://nananwachukwu.github.io/emfa-creator-guide

## Understanding this work

This visualisation walks through the EMFA in three parts:

**Who does this law apply to?**

*It looks at the eight categories of actors regulated by the EMFA, from media service providers to device manufacturers.*

**When does a creator become a media service provider?** 

*I have used the five-element threshold analysis with the actual legislative text, detailed legal analysis, and provided practical creator tests for each element.**

**What protections does the law provide?**

*I have oulined the eight provisions that directly affect creators' relationship with very large online platforms, including pre-takedown notice, priority complaints, forced dialogue, transparency reporting, and audience measurement disclosure.*

All quoted text is verbatim from the regulation as published in the Official Journal of the European Union.

## Part of a Larger Research Work

This guide is part of the <a href="https://nananwachukwu.github.io/media-capture-watch/" target="_blank">Media Capture Watch</a> project, which maps funding relationships between technology companies and journalism organisations. Together, they show the gap between the protections the EMFA provides (focused on state capture of media) and the protections the information ecosystem actually needs (addressing Big Tech capture through financial entanglement).

Both projects are part of doctoral research at the **Trinity College Dublin**, examining how AI platforms construct and maintain power through the enclosure of accountability.

## Legal Reference

Regulation (EU) 2024/1083 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024, establishing a common framework for media services in the internal market and amending Directive 2010/13/EU (European Media Freedom Act).
